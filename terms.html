The short version

  • Direct Supply is a platform that helps schools and supply teachers find each other. We're not an agency and we don't employ or supply teachers.
  • Schools use it free. Teachers pay £20 a month or £199 a year to be listed, and can cancel at any time.
  • Schools pay teachers directly. We never handle that money and never take a cut.
  • Schools must still complete their own safer recruitment checks before a teacher starts.

2. What Direct Supply is, and isn't

Direct Supply is an online directory and messaging platform. Teachers list their availability and day rate. Schools search, message, post vacancies and send booking requests.

  • We are not an employment agency or employment business. We don't employ teachers, supply them to schools, set their pay, or take any part in the working arrangement.
  • Any engagement is agreed directly between the school and the teacher. That includes the terms, the pay and how it's paid (for example through the school's payroll or the teacher's umbrella company). We are not a party to it.
  • We don't guarantee that a teacher will find work, or that a school will find cover.

3. Accounts

  • You must be 18 or over, and the information you give us must be true and kept up to date.
  • One account per person. Keep your password safe; you're responsible for activity on your account.
  • School accounts must be created by someone who works at the school and is authorised to arrange supply cover for it.

4. Verification, and what it means

For teachers, we check photo ID and right to work, teacher registration (DfE, EWC, GTC Scotland or GTCNI), criminal record check status (DBS Update Service, PVG or AccessNI) and two references. For schools, we check the official school register and call the school office.

A "Verified" badge means those checks were completed at the time shown. Circumstances can change after a check, so verification by Direct Supply does not replace a school's own pre-employment and safer recruitment checks, including those required by Keeping Children Safe in Education and its equivalents in Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland. Schools remain responsible for deciding who works with their pupils.

We may refuse, suspend or remove verification where a check fails, information turns out to be false, or we have a safeguarding concern.

5. Teacher subscriptions

  • Being listed as a live teacher costs £20 a month or £199 a year. The price is shown before you pay.
  • Payments are taken by Stripe. Your subscription renews automatically at the end of each month or year until you cancel.
  • You can cancel at any time from Account → Manage billing. Your profile stays live until the end of the period you've paid for, and you won't be charged again.
  • If you're signing up as a consumer, you can cancel within 14 days of your first payment for a full refund by emailing us. Apart from that, payments for periods that have started aren't refundable, except where the law says otherwise.
  • If we change the price, we'll email you at least 30 days before it applies to you, and you can cancel before then.
  • If a payment fails and isn't fixed, your profile will be taken offline until it is.

Schools use Direct Supply free of charge.

6. Paying teachers

Schools pay teachers directly for the work they do. Direct Supply never holds, receives or forwards that money and takes no commission. Tax, National Insurance, pension and employment status are matters for the school and the teacher.
