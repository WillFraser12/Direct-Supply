Questions

How do teachers get paid?

The school pays the teacher directly, usually through the school's own payroll, or through the teacher's umbrella company. Direct Supply never holds the money and takes no cut.

What checks do you do on teachers?

We check photo ID and right to work, the teacher's registration (DfE, EWC, GTC Scotland or GTCNI), their criminal record check (DBS Update Service, PVG or AccessNI) and two references. Schools should still complete their own pre-employment checks before a teacher starts.

How do you verify schools?

We check each school against its nation's official school register, then ring the school office on the number listed there to confirm the person signing up works there.

Where does Direct Supply work?

Across England, Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland. Search by region, town or postcode.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time and stop at the end of the month you've paid for.
